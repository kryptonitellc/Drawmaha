# Privacy Policy for Drawmaha

**Last Updated: October 25, 2026**

## Overview

Drawmaha (the “App”) is an online heads-up poker game available on web and mobile. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Handle

Depending on how you use Drawmaha, we may collect and process:

- **Account information:** username, email address, password credential (stored as a hash), account type, profile picture choice, and account creation and activity timestamps. You can play as a guest without providing an email address; guest accounts receive a generated username.
- **Game and profile information:** match and hand history, actions and results, chip balance, Drawmaha rating, statistics, selected settings and cosmetic choices. Match records may be visible to the other player and some profile and rating information may appear in search, friend lists, or leaderboards.
- **Social and safety information:** friend requests and relationships, blocked players, player reports, invitations, and chat messages sent during a match.
- **Reminder and device information:** if you enable push reminders, the App stores a push notification token for your device. If turn reminder emails are enabled in Settings, we use your verified email address to send those reminders. The App also sends account verification, email change, and password reset messages when you request those actions.
- **Technical and security information:** authentication/session credentials, request and rate-limit records, and information needed to protect accounts and prevent abuse. The service processes network connection information, such as IP addresses, when handling requests and security controls.

We use this information to provide and operate the App, authenticate users, maintain matches and ratings, support social features, send requested or account-related messages, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service problems.

## Storage, Retention, and Account Deletion

Account and game data is stored on Drawmaha's servers so the App can work across devices and preserve matches. Some preferences and session credentials may also be stored on your device. Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords.

Match chat messages are retained for up to 30 days after a match is completed. Older detailed hand history may be moved to archive storage after one year; match summaries and the final hand of each game remain available online. Security records and reports may be retained for the time needed to protect the service, investigate abuse, or meet legal obligations.

You can request account deletion from the App's Settings. Deletion removes or anonymizes account details such as your email, password credential, username, and profile image, removes push tokens and social connections, and signs out your sessions. Completed match and hand records may remain in an anonymized form so the other player's game history and service records remain intact. Reports may be retained for safety review. Unfinished matches are ended when an account is deleted.

## Service Providers and Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share or make information available only as needed to operate the App, including:

- **Infrastructure providers** that host the application, database, and archived game history.
- **Resend**, which delivers account verification, email change, password reset, and turn reminder emails.
- **Expo**, which delivers push notifications to registered mobile devices when push reminders are enabled.
- **Other players and App users**, as required for matches, chat, profiles, friend features, and public rankings. Do not include sensitive personal information in your username or match chat.
- **Authorities or other parties**, when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce our terms, or address fraud and security issues.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Providers may process information in countries other than where you live.

## Analytics and Advertising

The mobile app uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads. When ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), ad and app interaction information, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. See [Google's description of data handled by the Mobile Ads SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Where required, Google's consent tools ask for and store your advertising privacy choices before ads are requested. You can revisit available ad privacy choices in the App's **Settings → Ad privacy** when that option is required for your location. You can also manage advertising identifiers through your device settings. If you do not consent to ads where consent is required, the App will not request ads when the consent state does not permit it.

## Your Choices

- You can use a guest account without adding an email address.
- You can manage turn reminder emails in Settings and manage push permissions through your device settings.
- You can update some profile and preference information in the App.
- You can delete your account through Settings. You may also contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to information we must retain for legitimate safety, legal, or operational reasons.

## Children's Privacy

Drawmaha is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will update the date at the top when we publish revisions. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide notice through the App or another appropriate method.

## Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the support email shown in Drawmaha's **Settings → About**.
